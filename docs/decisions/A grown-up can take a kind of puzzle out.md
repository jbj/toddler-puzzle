# A grown-up can take a kind of puzzle out

The fixed ramp spans abilities that one child may not acquire at the same pace.
A grown-up may therefore remove a whole kind of puzzle from the child's current
play without turning the play surface into a difficulty picker.

**The setting filters the level table; it never edits it.** Every progression
helper answers from the same filtered view, so moving forward, resuming,
detecting a boundary, and finishing the game remain coherent.

**The filter is per kind, not per chapter or individual level.** A grown-up's
question is whether the child is ready for that interaction, wherever it occurs
in the ramp.

**At least one kind remains playable.** The panel refuses a change that would
leave no game, and invalid stored settings recover to the full ramp.

**Nothing changes on the child's surface.** The controls stay behind the
grown-up panel's deliberate hold; skipped rows are stepped over through ordinary
forward progression. See
[Put the settings behind a deliberate hold](<Put the settings behind a deliberate hold.md>)
and
[Keep the game moving forward](<Keep the game moving forward.md>).

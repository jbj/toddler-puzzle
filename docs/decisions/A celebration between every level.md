# A celebration between every level

Every completed level earns a playable celebration. Progression boundaries get
the larger celebrations; other levels get a smaller interlude. A fanfare and an
immediately available next board are not enough of a break for a young child.

**The way onward appears after a fixed pause on every completion.** The pause
belongs to the seam between levels, not to one celebration animation. It gives
the finished board and new play a moment before the most conditioned control
returns.

The pause cannot become a trap:

- the celebration responds from its first frame, so only leaving is delayed;
- nothing advances by itself;
- if the celebration fails to appear, the way onward appears without an empty
  wait;
- only visible, attended time consumes the pause.

The timing value and interlude selection live in the celebration code. The
durable rule is that every completion receives the break and that the child can
play throughout it.

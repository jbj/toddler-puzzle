# A level names what it is made of

The difficulty table must make the ramp readable. If a kind chooses its subject
only at deal time, two rows can become the same puzzle - same kind, subject, and
size - without that duplication being visible in the table.

**A level is its kind, subject, and size, and no two rows share all three.**
Each row names the subject appropriate to its kind, and the level check rejects
a duplicate identity.

A subject may recur at a different size because a different cut or amount is a
different task for the child. What is forbidden is an exact repeat that adds
nothing to the ramp.

Naming the subject does not make the whole deal fixed. Eligible pieces and tray
order remain fresh; only the part that defines what the level *is* belongs in
the table.
